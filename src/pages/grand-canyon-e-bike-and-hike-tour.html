Itinerary for This Tour:

This private, active tour combines the best of the South Rim into one unforgettable day. Travel to the canyon aboard a comfortable interpretive touring van while learning about the region’s geology, history, wildlife, and culture from your expert guide.

Once at the Grand Canyon, enjoy a scenic 5-mile self-guided e-bike ride along the South Rim, stopping at spectacular viewpoints before arriving at a delicious picnic lunch. Then, lace up your hiking shoes for a guided descent on the iconic South Kaibab Trail, where you’ll venture below the rim for breathtaking panoramic views and a true appreciation of the canyon’s immense scale.

Perfect for active travelers, outdoor enthusiasts, families, and friend groups, this Grand Canyon bike and hike package offers an exciting combination of sightseeing, adventure, and unforgettable scenery—all in one incredible day.

Know Before You Go!

Important Hiking Information:

This is a strenuous hike that includes steep descents, uneven terrain, loose gravel, and exposed sections with significant drop-offs. Trekking poles are strongly recommended and can be provided upon request. Proper clothing and shoes is REQUIRED to do this hike. (Closed toed shoes that are worn in and socks, hat, sunscreen, shirt with sleeves that can be added over any tank tops/exposed arms.

Participants should be comfortable hiking on uneven surfaces and navigating loose footing. Those with a fear of heights may find certain sections challenging and should carefully consider whether this portion of the experience is right for them.

For safety reasons, this hike is not recommended for individuals who are pregnant or who have knee, heart, balance, or mobility concerns.

Children are welcome only if they are mature enough to consistently follow safety instructions, maintain self-control near exposed edges, and remain under the supervision and direction of guides and parents at all times.

If you have any concerns about your ability to safely complete this hike, please contact us before booking so we can help determine whether this experience is a good fit.
