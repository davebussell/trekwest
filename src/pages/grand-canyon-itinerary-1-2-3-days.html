The short version
  • One day: the full South Rim, Village to Desert View, with sunset if you can stay late.
  • Two days: add a hike below the rim or the e-bike day, and a proper sunset.
  • Three days: add Sedona and Oak Creek Canyon, or Wupatki and Sunset Crater, or Page.
  • Base in Flagstaff throughout. Everything below is 1.5 hours or less from your hotel.

One day: the South Rim, properly

Self-drive version. Leave Flagstaff by 7:30 am on US-180. Mather Point by 9. Walk the Rim Trail to the Village (2.5 miles) via Yavapai Geology Museum; shuttle back. Short walk down Bright Angel to the first tunnel. Picnic lunch at Grandview Point. Drive Desert View Drive stopping at Moran, Lipan and Navajo Points; climb the Watchtower. Either head home via Cameron and US-89 (a loop) or wait for sunset at Lipan Point and drive back in the dark (1.75 hours).

Guided version. We pick you up at 8 am, and the day tour covers the same ground with the park fee, the picnic and the parking handled, and a guide explaining what you are looking at. Back at your hotel around 5:30 pm, in time for dinner downtown.

Two days: add depth

  • Day 1: the one-day plan above, but skip sunset.
  • Day 2, choose one:
    • E-bike and hike day: the car-free Greenway along the rim by e-bike, then South Kaibab to Ooh Aah Point for a picnic.
    • Guided day hike to Cedar Ridge for people who want to get properly below the rim.
    • A slow morning in Flagstaff (coffee at Macy's, the Museum of Northern Arizona), then the sunset and stargazing tour departing at 2 pm. This is the combination we would pick.

Three days: add the neighbourhood

Days 1 and 2 as above, then one of:

  • Sedona and Oak Creek Canyon. Drive down SR-89A (45 minutes), hike West Fork or Cathedral Rock early, lunch in Sedona, back up the canyon in the afternoon light. Sedona hiking guide.
  • Wupatki, Sunset Crater and the Painted Desert. The 35-mile loop road north-east of Flagstaff: an 1,000-year-old volcano, 800-year-old pueblos, and the red edge of the Navajo Nation. Half a day, little walking, almost no crowds. We run this as a private East Rim and volcanoes day too.
  • Page. Horseshoe Bend and Antelope Canyon, 2 hours north. How to make that work.
  • Flagstaff's own trails. Old Caves Crater, Walnut Canyon, or the aspens in October.

Seasonal notes

  • Summer (June to August): be at the rim early; afternoon monsoon storms from July are dramatic and brief; hike only early or late.
  • Autumn (September to October): the best season. Warm days, cool nights, aspens turning near Flagstaff.
  • Winter (November to March): snow on the rim, empty overlooks, extraordinary light. Hermit Road opens to private cars. Bring layers and traction for icy paths.
  • Spring (April to May): windy but lovely; the river trips start running; Havasupai Gardens water turns on.
Pickup: hotel and vacation-rental pickup is included within Flagstaff city limits (and the Williams area on our Williams tour). We can often arrange other pickup points for an extra fee. Ask us before you book.
