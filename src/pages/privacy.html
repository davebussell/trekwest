TrekWest (“TrekWest,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a DBA of Neal Ventures LLC, an Arizona limited liability company. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when visiting trekwest.net, communicating with us, booking a tour, or using our services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices you have regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Billing or mailing information
  • Tour dates and reservation information
  • Number of guests and information necessary to coordinate your tour
  • Pickup location or lodging information when necessary for transportation
  • Information you provide through contact forms, booking forms, emails, phone calls, or other communications
  • Special requests or other information you voluntarily provide in connection with a tour

When payments are made online, payment information may be collected and processed by a third-party payment or booking provider. TrekWest does not necessarily receive or store your complete credit or debit card information.

2. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by our website platform, analytics providers, or other service providers. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages viewed
  • Referring website
  • Approximate geographic location based on IP address
  • Date and time of visits
  • Interactions with our website

This information helps us operate, secure, understand, and improve our website and marketing.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and third-party services integrated with it may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, or similar technologies.

Cookies are small pieces of information stored on your device that can help websites function, remember preferences, understand how visitors use a website, and measure the effectiveness of advertising or marketing.

You may be able to limit or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of our website.

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use information collected through our website or during the booking process to:

  • Respond to questions and inquiries
  • Process and manage tour reservations
  • Communicate regarding upcoming tours
  • Coordinate pickup locations, meeting locations, schedules, and other tour logistics
  • Process payments
  • Provide customer service
  • Send reservation confirmations and important trip information
  • Improve our tours, website, and customer experience
  • Analyze website traffic and usage
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or security issues
  • Maintain business and accounting records
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements
  • Send marketing communications when permitted by law

You may unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time using the unsubscribe instructions provided in the communication or by contacting us.

5. How We Share Information

TrekWest does not sell your personal information.

We may share information when reasonably necessary to operate our business or provide your tour, including with service providers that assist us with:

  • Tour reservations and booking
  • Payment processing
  • Website hosting
  • Email and communications
  • Analytics
  • Advertising and marketing
  • Accounting and business administration

Information may also be shared when required by law, legal process, government request, or when reasonably necessary to protect TrekWest, our customers, guides, property, or others.

When you book through a third-party travel agency, tour marketplace, booking platform, or other service, information collected by that company is also governed by that company’s own privacy policy.

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or integrate services operated by other companies.

TrekWest does not control the privacy practices of these third parties. If you leave our website or interact with a third-party booking, payment, mapping, social media, analytics, or other service, that company’s privacy policy and terms may apply.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party services you use.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect personal information under our control.

However, no website, electronic transmission, or data storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted through or stored in connection with our website.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, operate our business, maintain appropriate business and financial records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

Information that is no longer reasonably necessary may be deleted or anonymized when appropriate.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are intended for general audiences and are not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information online directly from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, please contact us so we can address the situation.

Parents or guardians may provide information regarding minor participants when booking a family or group tour when that information is reasonably necessary to provide the tour.
