The short version
  • Park entrance: $35 per private vehicle, valid 7 days; $20 per person on foot or bike; $30 per motorcycle. Cards only at the gate.
  • Non-US residents aged 16 and over currently pay an additional $100 per person unless they hold an annual pass; the $250 non-resident America the Beautiful pass covers a family in one car.
  • Lodging is the big variable: a Flagstaff hotel often costs half of Tusayan or the in-park lodges.
  • A guided day tour at $199 per adult includes the park fee, lunch, pickup and about six hours with a guide. Compare like with like.

Getting there

  • Rental car: market rate, plus fuel. Flagstaff to the rim and back is about 160 miles.
  • Parking: free inside the park, but the Village lots fill by mid-morning in summer.
  • Shuttles inside the park: free.
  • From Phoenix or Las Vegas: add 4 to 9 hours of driving per day. Phoenix guide, Las Vegas guide.

Food

In-park options run from the Maswik food court to the El Tovar dining room. Prices are resort-level. The sensible move is a packed lunch from Flagstaff: the picnic tables at Grandview and Lipan Point have the best views of any restaurant in Arizona. (Our tours include a Route 66 café picnic for exactly this reason.) Flagstaff dinners: our list.
