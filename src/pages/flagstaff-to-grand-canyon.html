The short version
  • Flagstaff to Grand Canyon Village: 80 miles / 130 km, about 1 hour 30 minutes via US-180 and SR-64.
  • The Williams route (I-40 west, then SR-64 north) is 10 minutes longer but plowed first in winter.
  • Flagstaff is the best base: more hotels and restaurants than Tusayan, cooler than Phoenix, and under 90 minutes from the rim.
  • Our day tours leave Flagstaff at 8 am and have you back by about 5:30 pm with park fees, lunch and the driving handled.

The drive

Head north-west out of Flagstaff on US-180 (Humphreys Street becomes Fort Valley Road). You pass the Museum of Northern Arizona and the Arizona Snowbowl turn-off, climb through ponderosa pine with the San Francisco Peaks on your right, then drop onto open grassland for the last 30 miles to Valle. At Valle turn right on SR-64 for the final 30 miles to Tusayan and the South Entrance. On a quiet morning it is 1 hour 20 minutes; allow 1 hour 45 minutes in summer when the entrance queue builds after 10 am.

Winter alternative: I-40 west 30 miles to Williams, then SR-64 north 60 miles. ADOT keeps the interstate and SR-64 clear first. Check az511.gov before you leave if snow is forecast.

Why we think Flagstaff beats staying at the canyon

  • Choice. Tusayan has a handful of hotels and chain restaurants, all at a premium. Flagstaff has a historic downtown, dozens of restaurants, breweries and every price of hotel. Our basecamp guide goes through the trade-offs.
  • Other days. Sedona, Walnut Canyon, Wupatki and Sunset Crater, Meteor Crater and excellent day hikes are all within an hour.
  • The price. Park entrance ($35 per vehicle) and the drive are the only extra costs, and on our tours both are covered.

Drive yourself or take a tour?

Driving yourself is easy and the road is good. What you lose is the knowledge: which overlooks are worth the stop (and which are three parking lots looking at the same view), where the quiet picnic spots are, what the rock layers are telling you, where the condors have been roosting this week. A guide also solves the summer problem of the Village parking lots being full by 10 am.

Our Grand Canyon day tour from Flagstaff picks up at your hotel at 8 am, caps at 10 guests, includes the park fee and a Route 66 café picnic lunch, and spends about six hours in the park across the Village, the Rim Trail and the Desert View overlooks. If you are staying in Williams, the Williams departure does the same loop.

Pickup: hotel and vacation-rental pickup is included within Flagstaff city limits (and the Williams area on our Williams tour). We can often arrange other pickup points for an extra fee. Ask us before you book.

Worth a stop on the way

  • Museum of Northern Arizona (3 miles out of town): the best introduction to the geology and the Colorado Plateau peoples you are about to see.
  • Kendrick Park meadow at dawn or dusk for elk.
  • Valle: the Planes of Fame air museum and the Flintstones-era Bedrock City remnants, for the kids and the kitsch.
