Enjoy a fully outfitted Grand Canyon day hike for your private group of up to 10 guests. Explore one of the South Rim’s most scenic trails alongside a seasoned local hiking guide, with expert support, gear, and personalized pacing throughout the day.

Itinerary for This Tour:

Grand Canyon should be at the top of your hiking bucket list – it’s EPIC!There’s only one real way to understand the true scale and depth of the Grand Canyon — and that’s by hiking below the rim. This private guided Grand Canyon hiking tour is the perfect way to experience the Canyon beyond the overlooks while staying safe, comfortable, and fully supported.

Whether you’re looking for a light scenic hike or a more challenging adventure, we customize the day around your group’s abilities, pace, and goals. Our expert guides provide the knowledge, gear recommendations, encouragement, and local expertise to make your hike unforgettable.

We typically guide hikes on the breathtaking South Kaibab Trail, famous for its dramatic panoramic views and incredible photo opportunities. Alternative routes like Bright Angel Trail or Hermit Trail can often be arranged upon request.

Your private 8-hour hiking adventure includes interpretive transportation from Flagstaff or Williams in a luxury touring van, with fascinating stories about the region’s geology, history, wildlife, and culture shared along the way.

Flat-rate pricing includes up to 10 guests. Larger groups are welcome — contact us for custom arrangements!

  • 8:00 am | Departure Depart with your private group from your Flagstaff accommodations (or 8:30-45 am for pickups in Williams). Enter park at the S. Rim entrance
  • 9:00-30 am | Grand Canyon Visitors Center| South Kaibab trail |We arrive at the Canyon and after a bathroom break we gear up and load the Park shuttle bus system. We will take our snacks/ water & lunches with us as we go to explore this wonder of the world!
  • 10:00 am Begin hike to Cedar Ridge – 3 miles (4.8 km) round trip|As we descend into the Canyon the geology comes to life. The trail offers multiple options to “rock talk” as we walk! TrekWest guides are great to provide the Grand Canyon geology information is a way that is easy to understand while being accurate and fun! (yes geology can be fun)
  • 12:00-1:30 pm | Picnic lunch trailside| Enjoy your delicious lunch, you’ve earned it. Nothing tastes better than a lunch with a great view. We offer time to explore our surroundings and take some photos. Cedar Ridge is outfitted with a pit toilet, in case you need the piece of mind.
  • 1:30 pm | Begin Return Hike to South Kaibab Trailhead | As we return to the Visitor center, we stop as much as needed to catch our breath. Going down the trail can be “easy”, but we have got to hike back out. The elevation drop to Cedar Ridge is 1,200ft (366 meters) so plan for drinking plenty of water and taking it one step at time. Your guide will bring extra water & snacks to ensure a safe hiking experience.
  • 3:30 pm | Complete the hike | As we top out, we’ll load the shuttle bus to head back to the vehicle. The visitor center is the location of Canyon Adventures bike shop & cafe. We can grab some refreshments and bathroom stop before departing back to area lodges in Flagstaff, Williams or Tusayan.
  • 5:30 pm | Day Hike Tour Complete at FlagstaffAs we drive back to Flagstaff or Williams, Your guide will share local favorites and recommendations for the best dinner spots in town to cap off your evening.
