Welcome to TrekWest!

TrekWest was founded by Wes and Sarah Neal after 14 years building and operating Bright Angel Bicycles at Grand Canyon National Park. Today it’s a family-owned boutique adventure company dedicated to helping travelers experience the Grand Canyon and Northern Arizona in a more personal, meaningful, and memorable way.

Capping out at 10 guests, our small-group tours stay small enough to ensure you fully experience the canyon’s history and geology without ever feeling crowded or hurried, and to take you to the South Rim’s most breathtaking ‘secret’ vistas away from the masses.
